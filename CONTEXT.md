# Domain glossary

**Third party charge (TPC):** a non-commodity electricity cost, such as DUoS, BSUoS or the CfD levy.

**Turn-down:** a reduction in an MPAN's import in one settlement period, in kWh at the meter (MSP). It will usually come from a battery, but the tool doesn't model the cause.

**Avoided cost / saving:** the TPC cost that no longer applies because of a turn-down. It uses one rate per TPC. Whether that rate is a forecast, industry or contract number is up to the data source.

**Firm saving:** a saving that later periods cannot change. This is the headline figure.

**Possible saving:** a saving that a later period could still remove. This applies to exceeded capacity until the month's HH data is complete.

**Agreed capacity:** an MPAN's Maximum Import Capacity in kVA. It is also called MIC.

**Exceeded capacity:** the amount by which a month's highest half-hourly kVA is above agreed capacity. It is charged per kVA for every day of that month.

**Excess reactive:** kVArh above 33% of kWh in a half hour, which is equivalent to a 0.95 power factor.

**Capacity Market peak window:** 16:00 to 19:00 UK time on Working Days, November to February.
